GOAL:
The exploration goal is which type of shows to produce and how to grow the business.

There are 2 files Business_Case_Study_Netflix and Netflix_data_exploration_business_case_samyu , the first file has basic analysis and the second file has deeper analysis giving a better understanding, after trying the basic analysis, you can go for the advanced analysis and get a deeper understanding of the datasets and the current metrics of the streaming platform.

Netflix Data Analysis – Business Insights & Growth Strategy Gathered, cleaned, and analyzed a dataset of 10,000+ titles to extract actionable insights supporting Netflix's content strategy. Built and maintained data pipelines to ensure data quality and availability for analysis. Examined content distribution across 100+ countries, identifying high-demand regions to inform localized content production. Analyzed 30+ years of release data to uncover shifts in focus between movies and TV shows, aligning with evolving viewer preferences. Compared movies vs. TV shows in terms of volume, duration, and ratings to optimize content investment strategies. Developed data analysis plans and created compelling visualizations to communicate findings to stakeholders. Collaborated with cross-functional teams to translate insights into strategic recommendations for business growth. Applied latest data analysis techniques and tools to identify trends and support data-driven decisions.
